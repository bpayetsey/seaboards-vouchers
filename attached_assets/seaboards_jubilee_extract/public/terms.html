Terms & Conditions

1.The Offer

  1. To mark 50 years of Seychelles Independence, The Seaboards Apartments is releasing a limited allocation of Golden Jubilee Stay Vouchers at specially reduced rates, payable over three (3) monthly instalments.
  2. Each voucher is sold per room, per night, on a Half Board basis, and is fully stackable — guests may purchase as many nights as they wish, subject to availability and the blackout dates set out in Clause 6.
  3. The minimum purchase is one (1) night.
  4. The Golden Jubilee rates are as follows:

All rates are quoted per room, per night, in Seychelles Rupees (SCR).

2.What Is Included

  1. Half Board for the named adult occupants — daily breakfast and a two-course dinner at the in-house restaurant.
  2. Children stay free of charge on a shared-room basis with an extra bed provided, where room capacity allows.
  3. A breakfast supplement of SCR 295 per child, per day applies for each child taking breakfast.
  4. Children's dinner is available à la carte at separate cost and is not included in the Half Board package.
  5. Beverages are not included unless expressly stated in writing.

3.The Golden Fifty Perk

  1. The first fifty (50) Jubilee Vouchers sold will receive one (1) complimentary cocktail or mocktail per adult (two adults), served with dinner.
  2. The Perk is allocated strictly in order of completed booking and is limited to the first fifty (50) vouchers sold. It applies on one (1) evening of the stay, has no cash value, and is non-transferable.

4.Payment & Voucher Issue

  1. Guests may pay for their voucher(s) in either of two ways:
  • Pay in full at the time of booking; or
  • Pay over three (3) equal monthly instalments.
  1. Where payment is made in full, the voucher is issued immediately upon receipt of cleared payment.
  2. Under the instalment plan, Instalment 1 is payable at the time of booking and secures the Jubilee price. Instalments 2 and 3 fall due at 30 and 60 days after the booking date respectively.
  3. Where payment is made by instalments, the voucher is issued only once the third (final) instalment has cleared in full. No voucher is valid, and no stay may be redeemed, until full payment is received.
  4. Illustrative instalment schedule (single night):

5.Missed or Incomplete Payments

  1. If any instalment is not received by its due date, The Seaboards will attempt to recover the payment by issuing up to three (3) payment reminders over a grace period of fourteen (14) days from the missed due date.
  2. If the outstanding instalment is settled within the grace period, the payment plan continues unaffected and the schedule resumes as normal.
  3. If the plan is not brought up to date by the end of the grace period, the booking is cancelled, no room voucher is issued, and all amounts already paid are converted into non-refundable Ezzy Group Credit.
  4. Ezzy Group Credit may be redeemed against either of the following sister services:
  • Ezzy Foods — dining at the Ezzy Foods restaurant; or
  • Ezzy Courier “Shop & Ship” — shipping of goods from the United Arab Emirates to Seychelles (ezzycourrier.com).
  1. The credit is equal in value to the total instalments paid, is valid for twelve (12) months from the date of conversion, is non-refundable and non-transferable, and may not be exchanged for cash.
  2. No room amount paid under this offer is refundable in cash under any circumstances. The Jubilee rate is a non-refundable promotional rate.

6.Validity & Blackout Dates

  1. Vouchers are sold from 29 June 2026 until 30 September 2026, or until the limited Jubilee allocation is exhausted, whichever occurs first.
  2. Issued vouchers are redeemable for stays up to and including 30 June 2027, subject to availability.
  3. Vouchers may not be redeemed for stays falling, in whole or in part, within the following blackout periods:
  • 15 July 2026 – 31 August 2026 (inclusive)
  • 20 December 2026 – 15 January 2027 (inclusive)
  1. Stays that overlap a blackout period, even partially, are not permitted under this offer.

7.Availability & Booking

  1. All stays are subject to availability at the time the voucher is redeemed. Purchasing a voucher does not, in itself, guarantee a specific date; guests are encouraged to confirm their stay dates as early as possible.
  2. Vouchers are subject to a minimum of one (1) night and are valid for the apartment type purchased only. A One-Bedroom voucher may not be applied to a Two-Bedroom apartment, or vice versa.

8.Date Changes & Transfers

  1. Once a voucher is issued, the guest may request a change of redemption dates subject to availability and the blackout dates. The Seaboards will use reasonable efforts to accommodate requested dates but does not guarantee any specific date.
  2. Vouchers are issued to the named purchaser and may not be re-sold. They may be gifted to a third party only with the prior written agreement of The Seaboards.
